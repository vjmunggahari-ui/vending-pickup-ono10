One pickup line, 200 characters at most, as a vending machine would say it to a passing human: a machine offers its last chocolate bar, saved for someone special, and the coil — the dumb spiral that drops the snack — has just picked you.

> Take the last chocolate bar. I was saving it for someone special, and the coil just picked you.

`main.ts` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` and nothing else.
